# Decisions

Calls made since the plan was approved, oldest first. Add an entry when you make a choice someone could reasonably have made differently. A call that needs a long write-up gets its own file in `docs/`, linked from its entry. A call that changes the plan is also edited into `GAMEPLAN.md`.

An entry is a heading with the date and a short name, then three lines:

- *Call:* what was decided.
- *Why:* the reason, including what was ruled out.
- *Status:* **settled**, or **to confirm** when the brief left it open and the driver may want it the other way. Entries still to confirm are listed in the report at ship.
